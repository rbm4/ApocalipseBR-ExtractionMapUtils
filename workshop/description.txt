[h1]ApocalipseBR - Extraction Map Utils[/h1]

Build 42 event lobby utilities. Reveal the world map and minimap regardless of base map sandbox settings, and relocate new and returning characters to a configurable lobby on every login.

Configure Lobby X, Lobby Y and Lobby Z under ApocalipseBR - Extraction Map Utils in sandbox settings. X and Y default to -1: relocation waits until a lobby is configured.
